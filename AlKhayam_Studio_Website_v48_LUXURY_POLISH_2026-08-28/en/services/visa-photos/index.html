U.S. visa photo

51 × 51 mm
Printed size51 × 51 mm
Photo ageTaken within the last 6 months and reflecting your current appearance.
BackgroundPlain white or off-white.
PoseFull-face, directly facing the camera, neutral expression and both eyes open.
Head height25–35 mm from chin to top of head.
EyeglassesNot permitted except in rare documented medical circumstances.
Digital imageSquare, 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels, JPEG.

35 × 45 mm
