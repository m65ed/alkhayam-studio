Frequently asked questions

What is the reference size for Saudi ID and passport photos?

The documented reference size is 40 × 60 mm. If the receiving authority provides different or newer instructions, those take priority.

Do you prepare Schengen, U.S. and Canadian visa photos?

Yes. See the visa photo page for sizes, key requirements and direct official sources.

Are the listed services available at all branches?

The services shown on the website are available across all eight Al Khayam Studio branches. Some specialised work may require central preparation.
