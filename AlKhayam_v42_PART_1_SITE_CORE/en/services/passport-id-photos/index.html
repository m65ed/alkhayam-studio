Passport & ID Photos in Al Khobar, Dhahran & Dammam

Official photographs require accurate dimensions, background and facial positioning. Al Khayam Studio prepares photos for common government and personal-document uses, while the receiving authority’s current requirements always take priority.

Choose a branchContact us
01

Saudi reference size

The documented reference size for Saudi passport, ID, driving licence, residency, school and medical-form photos is 4×6 cm. Bring any different specification issued for your application.

02

Before the photo

Bring the receiving authority’s instructions if you have a specific form or specification. The photographer assists with positioning and you select the preferred shot before production.

03

Official acceptance

No studio can guarantee acceptance by an external authority. The receiving authority makes the final decision and requirements may change.

Important

Official requirements can change. If the receiving authority provides instructions, those instructions take priority. Neither the website nor the studio can guarantee acceptance by an external authority.
