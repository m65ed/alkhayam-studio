Photo Restoration & Scanning

Restoration, scanning and reprinting options organized by what happens to the image and what affects the result.

View details
Services

What can be done with an old photograph?

ServiceWhat it doesWhat affects the result
ScanningConverts a paper photograph into a digital file.Paper condition, original detail and scan quality.
RestorationRepairs visible defects and improves the image as far as the original allows.Tears, fading, missing detail and source quality.
ReprintingProduces a new print from a suitable file or stored image.File resolution and requested size.
Background changeReplaces the background when suitable for the image.Edge clarity and file quality.
Transparent PNGCreates a transparent background when the image supports a clean cutout.Separation between subject and background.
1Bring the original or best available file.
2The branch assesses what can realistically be improved.
3Choose scanning, restoration or printing.
4The result is prepared based on the source condition.
The original matters

Restoration cannot recreate detail that does not exist in the source. A clearer original provides more room for improvement.
