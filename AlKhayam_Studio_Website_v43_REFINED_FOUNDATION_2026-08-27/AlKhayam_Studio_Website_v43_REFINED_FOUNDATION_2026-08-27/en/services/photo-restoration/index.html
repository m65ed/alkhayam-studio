05 · Al Khayam Studio services

Photo Restoration, Scanning & Background Editing

Older photographs may need scanning, digital restoration or reprinting. We provide restoration, scanning and reprinting, plus background replacement and transparent PNG preparation depending on source quality.

Choose a branchContact us
01

Restoration and scanning

Old photographs can be scanned, visible damage can be restored and files can be prepared for reprinting depending on the condition of the original.

02

Reprinting

If a suitable saved studio image or source file is available, a reprint can be ordered in an available size.

03

Background and transparency

Background replacement and transparent PNG preparation are available depending on the image type and source quality.
