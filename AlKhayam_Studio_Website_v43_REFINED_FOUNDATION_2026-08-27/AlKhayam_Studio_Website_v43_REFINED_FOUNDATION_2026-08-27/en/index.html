FAQ

Questions we hear often.

Clear answers about visiting a branch, official photos, digital copies and other common requests.

View all questions ←
Do I need an appointment?

No appointment is required. You can visit any branch during opening hours.

Are the same services available at every branch?

Yes. The services shown on the website can be requested at all Al Khayam Studio branches. Some specialised preparation may be handled centrally, and the branch will confirm collection timing.

Can I choose my photo before printing?

Yes. You select and approve your preferred shot before editing and printing. Another shot can be taken before approval and production begin.

What if an authority rejects my photo?

Provide the rejection reason shown by the authority. We will review it and correct the image according to policy when the issue results from our preparation. Final acceptance remains with the receiving authority.

Can I receive a digital copy?

With photography and printing, you may request a digital copy of the selected photo. Digital-only service is available separately.

Do you photograph children and babies?

Yes, including official photos. The photographer assists with positioning, with a parent or responsible guardian present.
