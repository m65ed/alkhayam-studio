United States

U.S. visa photo requirements.

Printed size2 × 2 in (51 × 51 mm)
Photo ageTaken within the last 6 months.
BackgroundPlain white or off-white.
PoseFull-face, directly facing the camera, neutral expression and both eyes open.
Head height25–35 mm approximately.
EyeglassesNot allowed in current U.S. visa photo rules.
Digital imageSquare, 600 × 600 to 1200 × 1200 pixels.
